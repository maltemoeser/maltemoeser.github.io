# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People who have just encountered Malte's name and want to place it: recruiters and hiring
managers, prospective collaborators, fellow researchers, journalists, and acquaintances who
Googled him. They arrive with a specific question ("who is this, and is he credible?") and
leave within a minute. Most come from a link (LinkedIn, a paper, a talk) on either desktop
or phone. They are scanning, not reading.

## Product Purpose

A personal site that establishes professional credibility at a glance. It states who Malte
is (staff software engineer at Chainalysis, PhD in Computer Science from Princeton), what he
works on now (the data engineering behind blockchain analysis at scale, and the AI/LLM
systems built on top of it), and backs it with a short list of selected publications. The
positioning leads with current senior engineering work; the research record is supporting
evidence, not the headline. Success is a visitor forming an accurate, favorable impression
in seconds and finding the one thing they came for: a way to reach him, his work, or his
background.

## Brand Personality

Restrained, precise, confident. The voice of someone who lets the substance carry the
weight and trusts the reader to be smart. Three words: calm, exact, unshowy. The page should
feel like quiet competence, not self-promotion. No hype, no hedging. Generous whitespace and
quiet typography over visual noise.

## Anti-references

- **Generic AI landing page**: gradient hero, hero-metric template, identical card grids,
  tiny tracked uppercase eyebrows above every section.
- **Corporate SaaS template**: Bootstrap/Notion-startup look, feature-card rows, marketing
  buzzwords.
- **Overdesigned academic CV**: cluttered, dense, every-publication-listed, sidebar-heavy
  university-homepage clutter.
- **Crypto / web3 aesthetic**: neon-on-black, 3D coins, glassmorphism, hype copy. The
  subject matter is blockchain analysis; the design must not borrow the industry's clichés.

## Design Principles

- **Substance over surface**: the credentials and publications are the content; design
  frames them, never competes with them.
- **Earn the visitor's second**: most arrivals last under a minute. Hierarchy must answer
  "who is this?" before any scroll.
- **Restraint is the statement**: in a field full of hype, quiet confidence is the
  differentiator. Whitespace and precision do the talking.
- **Every element justifies itself**: no decoration that doesn't carry information. If a
  visitor couldn't say why something is there, it shouldn't be.
- **Selective, not exhaustive**: a curated few publications signal judgment; a full CV dump
  signals the opposite.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Body text ≥4.5:1 contrast against its background; large text ≥3:1.
Semantic HTML and a logical heading order (the page is read by screen readers and scanned
fast). Honor `prefers-reduced-motion` for any animation. Fully responsive and legible from
phone to desktop. Link text meaningful out of context.
